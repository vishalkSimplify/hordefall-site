Hordefall Privacy Policy

Developer: Fogbank Games
App: Hordefall, Android package com.fogbankgames.hordefall
Effective date: October 10, 2026

Summary

  • Hordefall has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no user-generated content.
  • The game does not ask for location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • Your progress, coins, upgrades, settings and owned in-app items are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The game uses three Google services: Google AdMob (ads), Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) and Google Analytics for Firebase (gameplay statistics). These services collect some data as described below.
  • We do not sell personal data. We do not share data with anyone other than these Google services, which process it under their own policies.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device so you can pick up where you left off:

  • Game progress (your current level)
  • Coins
  • Upgrade levels
  • Settings (music, sound and haptics)
  • The IDs of the in-app products you own

This information stays on your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Information collected by third-party services

The game uses the Google services below. They collect and process data under their own privacy policies. We do not run our own servers for this data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch, and interstitial ads between levels. Ads are served by Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), your IP address (which gives a coarse location), device and app information, and data about how you interact with ads. Google uses this to serve and measure ads, to personalize ads where permitted, and to prevent fraud.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The game offers two in-app purchases: "Remove Ads" and "Starter Pack". Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details.

When you buy something, the app receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play (the product ID, a purchase token and an order ID) so it can unlock the item.

  • Google Payments Privacy Notice

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The game sends these gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase:

  • Session start
  • Level started, won or lost (with the level number)
  • Rewarded or interstitial ad shown
  • In-app purchase completed (with the product ID)

Firebase also automatically collects some data, such as an app-instance ID, device model, operating system version, language, country or region (derived from your IP address), app version, and first-open and session timing.

We use this only to understand how players progress through levels and to improve the game. We do not use it to identify you. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics storage is always turned off: Firebase receives only cookieless, aggregated measurement from those regions.

  • Privacy and Security in Firebase

Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ads are requested, the game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy choices.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, most requests are handled by uninstalling the app, resetting your Advertising ID, or using Google's own privacy tools. For anything else, contact us.

Children

Hordefall is not directed to children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help delete it.

Data retention and deletion

  • Data stored on your device remains until you uninstall the app, which deletes it.
  • Analytics data is kept according to Firebase's retention settings, for up to 14 months.
  • Data collected by AdMob and Google Play is kept according to Google's own policies, linked above.

Security

Data sent from the app to Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
